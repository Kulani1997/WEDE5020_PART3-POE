About Jabu's T&H

Beauty, confidence, empowerment and self-love.

Our Background and History

Jabu's T&H is a small beauty business established in 2022 by a young Black South African woman with a passion for beauty and empowering women.

The business was inspired by the founder's personal struggle with low self-esteem due to acne and insecurities about her dark complexion.

She initially started learning makeup to cover her acne and improve her confidence. Her passion for makeup gradually developed into a business as she began offering makeup services to other women.

The business later expanded its services to include hair installations, hairstyling and nail services.

Today, Jabu's T&H operates two salon branches in Khayelitsha and Delft, Cape Town, and employs eight people.

Our Mission

To empower women by providing quality and affordable beauty services that enhance natural beauty, build self-confidence and make every woman feel comfortable in her own skin.

Our Vision

To become a recognised and trusted beauty brand in Cape Town and beyond by delivering excellent services, creating employment opportunities and inspiring confidence through beauty.

Our Values

  • Confidence and self-love
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Professionalism
  • Women empowerment
  • Inclusivity and respect
